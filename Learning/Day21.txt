WebHooks - Firstly i compared it with kafka as i have basic knowledge of it.
            - what i understood about WebHooks
            - Any action of the application can be used to trigger a webhook (for ex - an order placed in shopify , update in status).
            - We can subscribe to a webhook when an event trigger it will cause the webhook to send any data from one system to another system.
            - It directly sends a HTTP post call to another system. (here it is different from kafka)
            - Shopify WebHook- it provides many webhook which can use by graphQl admin api 


            - GraphQL Admin API - set of apis in shopify to deal with customers , products , inventory , and all related stuff together.
                  - To use webhook we need it.
                  - Shopify uses GraphQL instead of Rest APIs  
                  - Their are many webhooks which we can subscribe to in shopify. [ex bulk create , update , app uninstalled]
                  - These hooks can be used for oms and shopify integration



AWS SQS - Simple Queue Service 
        - what is does ? It uses a simple FIFO architecture. 
        - It make sure messages recived in system will be processed correctly.
        - Simple , Secured , Reliable , Cost-Effective.
        - Store now and process later is the core problem it solves.
        - Its standard is JSON Data. but we have string and xml as well.

Shopify Data Model -  [GraphQL Schema - shopify exposes the schema in form of objects unlike ] 
      1. A customer browsers a brand's products on a channel. (pos , shopify , e-com)
      2. Customer adds item to cart
      3. Customer initiates checkout.
      4. The customer pays for the contents in the cart , resulting in an order.
      5. The brand fulfills the order 

      Objects - 
            Products - represents individual items available for sale in online store.  [collections , locations]  related objects
            Collections - organizes products into group for easy browsing / processing  [products]
            Customer - buyer who creates account and make payments. [orders , gift cards , companies]
            Companies - Identifies business entities associated with customers for B2B transactions. [customers]
            Locations - Specifies physical locations where inventory is stored [Products] 
            Orders - Tracks individual transactions made by customers, including product details and payment information
                        [Products, Locations, Customers, Markets, Companies, Fulfillment services]
            Discounts - Defines promotional offers and price reductions applicable to specific products or collections
            Markets - Represents different geographic or customer segments targeted for selling products
            Fulfillment orders - Represents tasks needed to prepare and ship parts of an order [Orders]
            Fulfillment services - Manages third-party logistics and other services for shipping and handling orders [Products]
            Gift cards - Tracks the issuance and redemption of store credit in the form of gift cards [Customers]
            Selling plan - Selling plans in Shopify are predefined configurations that allow merchants to offer 
                              products through various purchase options, such as subscriptions, pre-orders, or installments
