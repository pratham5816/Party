how this path is build?

[/rest] is hardcoded for rest services in moqui

http://[host]:[port]/rest/[API_master]/[Mount_point]/[ResourcePath]

Mount_point = <resource xmlns:xsi="http://www.w3.org/2001/XMLSchema-instance"
                        xsi:noNamespaceSchemaLocation="http://moqui.org/xsd/rest-api-3.xsd"
                        name={"automobile"} version="1.0" require-authentication="anonymous-all">
                        here in this case name="automobile


ResourcePath =   <resource name="bikes">
                       <method type="post">
                           <service name="AutomobileService.create#Bike"/>
                       </method>
                   </resource> here in this case "bikes"
